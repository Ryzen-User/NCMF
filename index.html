Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our guiding principles

Mission, Vision, and Core Values of the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos – National Capital Region

Mission

To protect, promote, and advance the rights and well-being of Muslim Filipinos in the National Capital Region through:

  • Responsive programs and social services
  • Interfaith solidarity and peace-building
  • Empowerment of Muslim communities
  • Collaboration with government and civil society
Unity Peace Development Inclusivity

Vision

A progressive, peaceful, and resilient Muslim community in the National Capital Region, fully integrated in national development, with equal opportunities and respect for their cultural and religious identity.

We envision a society where Muslim Filipinos are empowered, united, and contributing meaningfully to the nation's growth.
