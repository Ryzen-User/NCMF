Solemnizing Officer Accreditation

Apply for accreditation as a Solemnizing Officer authorized to perform legal marriage ceremonies for Muslim couples

List of Requirements

Please prepare the following documents before filling out the application form:

  • 1 Barangay Certificate as Imam for a certain Mosque
  • 2 The said Mosque is duly recognized by NCMF
  • 3 Endorsement from the Administrators or Jammaah of the said Mosque
  • 4 Arabic School Credentials
  • 5 Certificate of Authorization to Solemnize Marriage, if any
  • 6 Application Form duly accomplished by the applicant

Please ensure all documents are complete and up-to-date. Incomplete applications will not be processed.

Download Application Form

Download the official Solemnizing Officer Accreditation Form. Fill it out completely and submit it to the NCMF NCR office along with the required documents.
